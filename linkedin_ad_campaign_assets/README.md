## 2. LinkedIn Campaign Manager Upload Checklist

1. **Select Campaign Objective**:
   - For Brand Awareness / Reach: *Brand Awareness* or *Website Visits*.
   - For Lead Generation / Inquiries: *Lead Generation* (native LinkedIn Lead Gen Forms) or *Website Conversions*.
2. **Ad Format Selection**:
   - Single Image Ad: Use assets from `02_Single_Image_Ads_Landscape_1200x627/` (desktop) or `03_Single_Image_Ads_Square_1080x1080/` (mobile).
   - Carousel Ad: Select *Carousel Ad* and upload 2 to 10 cards from `04_Carousel_Ad_Cards_1080x1080/`.
   - Native Video / GIF: Select *Video Ad* or upload directly from `05_Video_and_Animated_GIFs/`.
3. **Copy & Text Configuration**:
   - Open `01_Strategy_and_Targeting/AD_COPY_VARIANTS.md`.
   - Select the Persona matching your audience (e.g., Persona 1 for Defense GEOINT, Persona 2 for Commercial SAR, Persona 4 for Infrastructure).
   - Paste the **Introductory Text** (keep above the fold, ~150 chars before "...see more").
   - Paste the **Headline** (keep under 70 characters).
   - Paste the **Description** (keep under 100 characters).
4. **Destination URL & UTM Tracking**:
   - Target URL: `https://viewerapp.vercel.app`
   - Include the validated UTM query string from the persona table (e.g. `?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=earth2_sar_q4&utm_content=sar_operators_hero`).
5. **Call To Action (CTA)**:
   - Select **"Explore"**, **"Request Demo"**, or **"Learn More"**.

## 3. Scientific Integrity Guarantees

In accordance with strict scientific guidelines (`.agents/rules/e2s-014-scientific-integrity.mdc`):
- **0% Synthetic Noise**: No procedural or pseudo-random noise was injected into any visual asset or data layer.
- **Physical Atmospheric Modeling**: Atmospheric phase delay is calculated via full physical numerical integration of Zenith Hydrostatic Delay ($N_{dry}$) and Zenith Wet Delay ($N_{wet}$) derived from authentic NOAA GFS 0.25-degree pressure and humidity tensors.
- **Ground Deformation Fidelity**: Crustal and anthropogenic subsidence signatures (aquifer compaction, karst dissolution, permafrost thaw) are modeled using elastic half-space and geodetic Okada formulations.
